| Busy Box Final Report.pdf | A marketing report using demographic comparisons, personas, forecasts, and recommendations | Develop guidance on compatible denominators, evidence-based personas, concise reporting, and recommendation traceability |

Student work must not be reproduced or publicly identifiable without explicit permission. New examples should use synthetic or openly licensed data.

The two supplied files named PLUS - DSCI 490 Final Report.pdf and PLUS - DSCI 490 Final Report(1).pdf are byte-for-byte duplicates, so they are treated as one source. The instructor described this report set as work receiving approximately 60 percent. It is therefore used diagnostically, not as a model answer.
