Learning goals

After working through the book, readers should be able to:

  • translate a broad organizational concern into useful analytical questions;
  • define project scope, deliverables, success criteria, responsibilities, and risks;
  • document and protect data throughout its lifecycle;
  • conduct and interpret a comprehensive exploratory data analysis;
  • combine primary data with credible and compatible secondary evidence;
  • select, justify, validate, and explain analytical and machine-learning methods;
  • construct defensible forecasts and projections and communicate their uncertainty;
  • develop recommendations that follow logically from evidence;
  • design realistic implementation and evaluation plans; and
  • communicate technical work to both specialist and nontechnical audiences.
