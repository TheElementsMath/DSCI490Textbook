17.8 EDA report outline

  1. purpose, questions, and scope;
  2. source data, versions, and units of analysis;
  3. data-quality findings and decisions;
  4. cleaning, transformation, and feature engineering;
  5. selected univariate findings;
  6. purposeful bivariate findings;
  7. relevant multivariate findings;
  8. limitations and unresolved issues;
  9. conclusions and project implications; and
  10. dictionary, references, code, and reproducibility information.
