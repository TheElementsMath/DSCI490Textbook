14.12 Quality assurance

Before delivery, reconcile:

  • values across code, tables, report, slides, and appendices;
  • category names and definitions;
  • time periods, filters, and denominators;
  • forecast and scenario labels;
  • recommendation claims and supporting evidence;
  • figure sources and citations;
  • confidentiality and disclosure review; and
  • file names, links, permissions, and final versions.

One person should perform a final cross-document review rather than assuming that separate section reviews guarantee consistency.
